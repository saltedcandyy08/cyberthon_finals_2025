After hours of digging, you finally unearth the treasure chest. Inside, gleaming with the promise of victory, is the flag. Just as you reach for it, an older guy in a white lab coat pops out of nowhere, snatches it, and tosses it into a blender. "Will it blend? That is the question." he asks with a mischievous grin.

The blender roars to life, and in seconds, your precious flag is shredded into a million tiny pieces. The guy shrugs, "Oh, I guess it does. Oops..." and hands you the blender full of flag confetti.

With the flag in tatters, you stare at the mess in horror, wondering if you can piece it back together. It's time to channel your inner hacker and reverse engineer the blender's work.

Can you salvage the flag?
