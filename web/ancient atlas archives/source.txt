Welcome to Ancient Atlas Archives, the most renowned map shop in town.

As you step into the shop, a voice echoes from the back, "Ah, a seeker of maps, are you? We have charts leading to buried treasures, sunken ships, and even the secret cookie stash of the local bakery. If perusing maps in a quaint, old store isn't your style, we have a splendid website for your convenience."

Your eyes are drawn to a dusty shelf marked "Secret Treasure Maps" with a single intriguing map placed on it. Curiosity piqued, you inquire, "What's that?" The voice responds, "Ah, that one is reserved exclusively for VIPs and our most loyal patrons. No touching!"

Undeterred, you attempt to persuade the cartographer for just a tiny glimpse, but your efforts are in vain. Can you find a way to sneek a peak at details of the map?
