A rival pirate crew is known to use a launchpad to remotely access their Windows Server vault.
One of your capable mates managed to sneak in to retrieve an account to login to the launchpad.

Your challenge: Login to the launchpad and comb the file system for forensics artefacts that the rivals left behind.
Bring those artefacts back for analysis, and you will be able to _see_ what they were doing in the vault and get your flag.

Rules: Exploits are not required, do not run any of them.
Denial of serivce attacks are not allowed.
